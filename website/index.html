How it works

Live answers from content you already have

No training, no data export. Agentyllo reads your site and keeps its knowledge fresh automatically.

Install & activate

One click from WordPress.org. It works instantly, on any hosting, behind any cache.

It reads your site

Pages, posts, products, menus and settings become an automatic, self-updating knowledge base.

Visitors ask

A modern, accessible chat widget understands the question and searches your content.

It answers — honestly

Prices, stock and contacts are quoted verbatim from your data. It links real pages, never bluffs.

Classic — instant & reliable

Pure-PHP agents answer from your content with no AI model at all. Zero cost, zero setup, runs anywhere. This is the floor every other mode builds on.

Powerful by design

Everything you need, nothing locked

The free plugin is complete. Optional add-ons only ever add capabilities on top.

Automatic knowledge base

Delta sync and nightly reconciliation keep answers fresh. Disable a source and it disappears from replies immediately.

Fact-guard, no hallucinations

Prices, stock, phone numbers and links come verbatim from your content. A structural guard rejects any answer your data doesn't back.

Bring your own AI

Free local engine (llama.cpp/Ollama) or your own OpenAI/Anthropic key. Streamed answers, a monthly cost cap, and a circuit breaker that always falls back to classic.

Backend copilot

Manage the knowledge base, settings and memories by command — every change is proposed first and applied only when you confirm.

GDPR & EU AI Act ready

Consent evidence, retention, PII redaction, data export/erasure, an assistant disclosure badge and a generated transparency page.

WooCommerce & Elementor aware

Live product prices and stock via WooCommerce; Elementor content indexed cleanly. Cache-safe on any host.

It answers like it read your whole site — because it did.

Real question, real answer, the exact fact quoted from your content with a link to the source. This is the classic engine — no AI key needed.
